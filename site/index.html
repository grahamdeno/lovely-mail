lovely

Get through your email, calmly.

Lovely turns your Gmail into a short list of next steps, walks you through each decision one at a time, and files everything neatly behind you. Gmail stays the filing cabinet; Lovely is the desk where you deal with your mail.

Waiting on you

See who's waiting, and on what.

When a person asks you something and you haven't answered, Lovely puts it at the top with their question in their own words. Reply and it moves on. Newsletters, receipts and do-not-reply notices stay out of it.

People

One timeline for each person.

Everything you and someone have written each other, in one place, with a short version at the top. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, that summary is written on the phone itself.

You stay in charge

Nothing happens without your tap.

You see it firstEvery change shows what it will do before you tap, and waits 10 seconds so you can undo it. Replies send only when you tap Send.
It never deletes for goodTrash means Gmail's Trash, which keeps mail 30 days. Filed mail gets a Lovely label and leaves your inbox.
It has no serverYour mail goes from Google to your phone and stays there. On-device Apple Intelligence by default; Claude or Grok only if you add your own key.
