Privacy.

Short version: your mail goes from Google to your phone and stays there. Lovely Mail has no server, no account of its own, no ads and no tracking.

What Lovely Mail can do with your Gmail

When you connect Gmail, Google asks you to allow Lovely Mail to read, organize and send mail for you. Lovely shows you what each change will do and waits for your tap, then holds it 10 seconds so you can undo it. It uses that permission to:

  • Read your inbox and recent sent mail to build Today and your inbox session.
  • Mark messages read when you finish them, and unread again if you undo.
  • File messages: add a label that starts with "Lovely/" (such as Lovely/Money) and take the message out of your inbox.
  • Create Gmail filters you ask for, such as always filing or screening out one sender. You can remove them in Settings.
  • Move messages to Gmail's Trash, and back if you undo. Gmail keeps Trash for 30 days. Mail you mark "Keep 2 weeks" moves to Trash on its own after 14 days; you can turn that off in Settings.
  • Send replies you approve, held 10 seconds so you can undo, and forward only to addresses you added in Settings.

It never deletes mail for good, and never changes labels it did not create or any other Gmail settings.

What stays on your phone

The mail Lovely loads, the short summaries it writes, and your choices (hidden messages, sender rules, your other addresses) are saved in the app on your iPhone. That file is protected while your phone is locked and is not included in iCloud or computer backups. Your Google sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. Disconnect Gmail clears the saved mail.

Apple Intelligence

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, summaries and the check on "Waiting on you" run on Apple's on-device model. Your mail is not sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone else to do it.

Claude and Grok (optional, off by default)

You can choose Claude (Anthropic) or Grok (xAI) for conversation instead of Apple's on-device model. They are off until you pick one and add your own API key. When one is on, the email you are working on and what you type about it are sent to that company under your key, to answer you. Mail that looks like health mail is never sent to them. Turn them off in Settings > Conversation engine.

What leaves your phone

Requests to Google, to load your mail and make the changes above. And, if Company logos is on, a request to a company's own website for its icon, using only the web address from the sender's email (never for people or shared services like gmail.com). That website sees the request, like any visit. Turn Company logos off in your profile under Pictures and Lovely makes no such requests.

If you allow Contacts, Lovely matches photos to email addresses on your phone. Nothing from Contacts leaves it. "Text someone about this" uses Apple's contact picker (Lovely sees only the number you pick) and opens Messages; you send the text yourself.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics sent anywhere, and no tracking across apps and websites. No selling or sharing data. We have no server holding your mail, so there's nothing for us to read, use for advertising, or use to train AI models.

Google API Services

Lovely Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

If you email us

If you write to support@tangomike.app, we get your email address and what you wrote. We use them only to answer you, and we'll delete the thread if you ask.

Deleting your data

Tap Disconnect Gmail, or delete the app, to remove everything Lovely saved on your phone. To end its access on Google's side, remove Lovely Mail at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Apart from any email you send us, we keep nothing.
